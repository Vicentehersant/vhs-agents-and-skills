# CRO Audit

## Framework (in evaluation order)
1. **Clarity (5-second test)**: does the hero answer what / for whom / why you in
   5 seconds? One primary CTA above the fold, visually dominant.
2. **Friction**: steps to convert, form field count, login walls (removing a login
   wall from a quote flow is usually a direct win), dead-end pages with no next action.
3. **Motivation**: benefit-led headlines (outcome, not feature), social proof near
   decision points (testimonials, logos, numbers), risk reversal (guarantees, free step).
4. **Trust**: real contact info, licenses/credentials, payment logos, professional
   imagery, consistent brand.
5. **Attention flow**: F/Z pattern respected, no competing CTAs, whitespace guides
   the eye, carousels don't hide key content.

## Recurring conversion patterns
- Quote/lead flows are often THE conversion action — reduce their friction obsessively.
- "Home cards → money page" direct routing (cards link to the converting page, not
  an intermediate hub).
- Sticky CTA on mobile (bottom bar or floating).
- Landing variants per audience reuse home sections, adapted — never thin doorway pages.

## Output format
Score each area 1–5 + table: element · problem · hypothesis ("changing X will
improve Y because Z") · effort (S/M/L) · expected impact (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW).
Implement quick wins first; propose A/B-worthy changes separately.
